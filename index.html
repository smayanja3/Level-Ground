WHERE GRIT MEETS OPPORTUNITY

At Level Ground (LG), urban youth cultivate grit and resilience through Mixed Martial Arts. They learn to apply these values 'off the mat' in the pursuit of their ambitions through our Student Trainer Certification and College Access programs.

WHY WE EXIST

Many urban youth face systemic barriers that limit their economic mobility and physical wellness. At Level Ground, we believe martial arts is the ultimate vehicle for personal growth. By combining rigorous physical training with academic support and employment opportunities, we empower young people to build self-confidence, unlock their potential, and rewrite their future trajectories.

VISIT THE GYM

Ready to push your limits? Step onto the mats at our flagship location. We offer community-focused classes in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Kickboxing, and Mixed Martial Arts for all ages and skill levels. Experience a supportive environment where community members and youth train side-by-side to develop physical strength and mutual respect.
